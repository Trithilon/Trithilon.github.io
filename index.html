MyLifeOS

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

MyLifeOS (together with its companion tools Holdfast and Zara) is a private, single-user project. It helps its owner work with their own email, calendar, contacts and files, and it is not offered to the public: there are no accounts to sign up for and no other users.

When it connects to a Google account, it does so only with the owner's permission, through Google's standard sign-in, and only to do what the owner asks.
