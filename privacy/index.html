MyLifeOS

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

MyLifeOS, Holdfast and Zara ("the app") are personal tools used by one person, their developer, on their own computer. This policy explains what the app does with Google user data.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app may access:

  • Google Drive — to search, read, create, organise and share the owner's files.
  • Gmail — to read, organise and send the owner's email.
  • Google Calendar — to read and manage the owner's events.
  • Google Contacts — to look up the owner's contacts.

It only uses this access to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

Where the data goes

  • Sign-in tokens and any data the app keeps are stored on the owner's own computer. There is no MyLifeOS server or cloud database.
  • To answer a request, the app may pass the relevant data to an AI model chosen by the owner — usually one running on the same computer, and sometimes a cloud AI service the owner has selected. Google user data is not used to train AI models.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except as the owner directs (for example, sending an email they asked the app to send).

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from their Google account permissions, and can delete the locally stored tokens and data from their computer.
